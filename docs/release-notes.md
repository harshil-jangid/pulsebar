## PulseBar 1.0

First release.

- Live heart rate in the menu bar from any standard Bluetooth heart-rate device (incl. Fitbit Air with *Share heart rate*), zones, 10-minute chart, auto-reconnect
- Desk sessions, workouts and a gentle elevated-at-desk nudge
- Optional Google Health sync (bring your own free OAuth client): resting HR, sleep HRV, sleep, steps, workouts
- Morning readiness vs. your own baseline, goal countdown and progress charts
- 10-second evening check-in and habit associations
- Read-only Claude connector (MCP)
- Export / delete everything; no accounts, servers or analytics

**Install:** open the `.dmg`, drag PulseBar to Applications, open it, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Open Anyway** (see README).
Requires macOS 26+. Not medical advice.
